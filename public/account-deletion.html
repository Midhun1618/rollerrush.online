PLAYER DATA & PRIVACY

ACCOUNT DELETION.

You can request deletion of your Roller Rush anonymous game account and the online player data associated with it. You do not need a conventional login to make a request.

HOW TO REQUEST DELETION

Send an email to our support address with the subject line “Roller Rush Account Deletion Request”.

✉   voxcom.online@gmail.com   ↗

Include your in-game username, if available, and any details that can help us identify your anonymous player profile. Do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive personal information.

WHAT DATA CAN BE DELETED?

After we identify the relevant profile, the request covers online Roller Rush data associated with it, which may include:

  • The anonymous Firebase authentication account.
  • The online player profile and username.
  • Leaderboard entries and best-distance records.
  • Online gameplay statistics and synced run records.

Local game data: Data stored only on your device, such as locally saved coins, settings, unlocks or progress, is not removed by an online deletion request. To remove local data, clear Roller Rush app storage in your device settings or uninstall the app.

WHAT HAPPENS AFTER I SEND A REQUEST?

We will review the request, identify the associated anonymous account and delete the online account data that we control. Some information may need to be retained where required for legal, security or operational reasons. We will explain any applicable retention when responding to your request.

For questions about this process, contact voxcom.online@gmail.com.
